EVIDENCE BOUNDARY
Talos directly reports PowerShell Add-MpPreference and reg.exe commands that
add C:\Windows\System32\inetsrv and C:\Windows\SysWOW64\inetsrv to Microsoft
Defender exclusions. The query detects those command patterns. A match does not
prove that the exclusion took effect, that malware was written to the excluded
directories, that SPECTRE or BadIIS executed, or that UAT-10147 is responsible.
