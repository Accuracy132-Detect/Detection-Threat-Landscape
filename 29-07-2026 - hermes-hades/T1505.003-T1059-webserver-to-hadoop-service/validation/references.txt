EVIDENCE BOUNDARY
Hunt.io recovered target-specific HiveServer2 tooling that opened a raw socket
to TCP 10000, registered a malicious Java UDF, issued commands through Hive SQL
and retrieved output through WebHDFS on TCP 50070. The researchers explicitly
recommend alerting on web-server processes connecting to those internal ports.

The validation query observes only network activity from inventoried web
servers to inventoried Hadoop nodes on the two source-observed service ports.
It does not establish a web shell, authentication, UDF registration, command
execution, data access, exfiltration, or campaign attribution.
