EVIDENCE BOUNDARY
Acronis directly reports a renamed Duplicate Files Finder executable loading
dupfdll.dll and then a malicious replacement mingwm10.dll from the same directory.
The query identifies that exact module combination within one process identity. A
match does not establish malicious file content, successful payload execution,
Grandoreiro attribution, credential theft, or C2 communication.
