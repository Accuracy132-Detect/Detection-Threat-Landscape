EVIDENCE BOUNDARY
The query searches file-system events for the official Cargo cache filenames.
A match shows that an affected or attacker-owned .crate artifact was observed
in a Cargo registry cache; it does not prove that Cargo compiled the crate, that
build.rs executed, that the payload was downloaded, or that secrets were stolen.
The C2 addresses, /tmp/rust-setup and stage-2 persistence artifacts are excluded
from this primary query because this package is limited to the official cache
check and avoids mixing evidence tiers.
